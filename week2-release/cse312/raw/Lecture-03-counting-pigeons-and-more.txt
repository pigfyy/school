Pigeonhole Principle: Strategy

To use the PHP to solve a problem, there are generally 4 steps

1. Identify pigeons
2. Identify pigeonholes
3. Specify how pigeons are assigned to pigeonholes
4. Apply PHP

Pigeonhole Principle – Example

In a room with 367 people, there are at
least two with the same birthday.




                                          52
Pigeonhole Principle – Example

In a room with 367 people, there are at
least two with the same birthday.


Solution:
1. 367 pigeons = people
2. 366 holes (365 for a normal year + Feb 29) = possible
   birthdays
3. Person goes into hole corresponding to own birthday
4. By PHP, there must be two people with the same birthday
