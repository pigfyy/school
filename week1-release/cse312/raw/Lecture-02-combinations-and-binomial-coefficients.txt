Quick counting summary from last class

• Sum rule:
      If you can choose from
           – EITHER one of 𝑛𝑛 options,
           – OR one of 𝑚𝑚 options with NO overlap with the previous 𝑛𝑛,
      then the number of possible outcomes of the experiment is 𝑛𝑛 + 𝑚𝑚

• Product rule:
      In a sequential process, if there are
           – 𝑛𝑛1 choices for the 1st step,
           – 𝑛𝑛2 choices for the 2nd step (given the first choice), ..., and
           – 𝑛𝑛𝑘𝑘 choices for the 𝑘𝑘th step (given the previous choices),
      then the total number of outcomes is 𝑛𝑛1 × 𝑛𝑛2 × 𝑛𝑛3 × ⋯ × 𝑛𝑛𝑘𝑘

• Representation of the problem is important (creative part)
